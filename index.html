A digital twin of five water-infrastructure testbeds

Replay the real evaluation periods of HAI 23.05, SKAB, Smart-Farm-IDS, WDSEventDB and GECCO 2018 through animated models of each plant. Choose any of the 14 detectors, its label budget and draw, and watch it raise or miss alarms as the attacks, faults and quality events unfold. Then switch to the what-if lab, inject your own faults and see detectors running in your browser respond.

About this digital twin

What is real. The sensor values are those of the evaluation periods of the five datasets, averaged over each 30-s hop of the 60-s analysis windows (6-min hops of 12-min windows on GECCO 2018). The anomaly scores are the stored per-window scores of the 1,055 benchmark runs: 14 detectors, every label budget k and label draw, and the zero-label references. Thresholds are the run's own: the F1-optimal threshold on the validation split (the default) or the 99th percentile of the validation negatives. Event recall, window precision, event F1, false alarms per hour of normal operation and detection delay are computed live; at the end of the replay they reproduce the recorded results of each run (for 7 of the 1,055 runs, all gradient boosting, a few windows lie within float32 rounding of the threshold, and the replay differs slightly).

What is modelled. The plant drawings are schematic. HAI P1 follows the physical boiler graph shipped with HAI; P2–P4 follow the HAI technical report. SKAB follows the benchmark's testbed description; the valve symbols close during the labelled valve-closing experiments. Smart-Farm-IDS, WDSEventDB and GECCO 2018 are laid out from the dataset descriptions and channel structure. Animations (levels, pump rotation, flows) are driven by the recorded values.

What is simulated. In the what-if lab, injected faults are synthetic changes of the recorded values. The 14 recorded detectors cannot see them (their scores are recorded); three detectors run in your browser instead: point deviation (largest |z|), PCA residual (squared prediction error of the normal correlation structure) and process-graph residual (every point predicted from its neighbours on the process graph). They use the same causal normal reference as the replay, are fitted on the normal record that precedes the evaluation period, and alarm at their 99th percentile there.

Study. B. Boukoftane, J. M. Navarro Ruiz, A. Pita Lozano. Graph Neural Networks versus Tabular Foundation Models for Label-Scarce Anomaly Detection in Water Infrastructure: A Comparison on Five Datasets. The results are preliminary and may change.
